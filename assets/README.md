# Paper Club assets

## Tabletop background

`paper-tabletop.png` is original artwork generated with the built-in image tool
on October 20, 2026 for this project. No reference image was edited. The welcome
board illustration and paper surfaces are drawn by the application at runtime.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: ui-mockup background asset, NOT a finished UI. Create a beautiful high-resolution landscape 16:10 background illustration for a warm tactile desktop board game called Paper Club. Only a stationery tabletop BACKGROUND, no app panels, no game board, no letters or numbers, no logos, no buttons, no mockup devices. Direct overhead orthographic composition. Soft warm oat paper resting on a cork desk, extremely subtle fine cream paper grain. Friendly boutique illustrated stationery style with believable soft shadows, not a busy photograph. Palette warm ivory #F4EDDC, sage green #85957A, ink navy #243F4A, small coral #DD6650 and cobalt #2E6190 accents. Keep the central 75 percent almost clear and low contrast so opaque interface paper sheets can sit on it. All objects sparse and small, partially cropped by the image edges: a few deep sage leaves peeking from the extreme upper-left and lower-right corners, a short blue wooden pencil near the extreme bottom-right edge, a tiny black binder clip near the lower-left edge, and slivers of layered sage and coral paper along the top-right edge. A sparse faint dotted stationery motif around the outer margin. Soft daylight, warm materials, calm depth, gentle playful craft. No full furniture, no people, no coffee cup, no large foliage, no central decorative object, no words, no border around the image. Background should read clearly as warm paper at a glance.

## Heading font

`fonts/Fredoka.ttf` is the Fredoka variable font from the
[Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka).
Its SIL Open Font License and copyright notice are retained in `fonts/OFL.txt`.
Body text uses the platform's Segoe UI font; that font is not redistributed.
